# ABC Technologies - Company Policy Document

Version: 1.0

---

## About ABC Technologies

ABC Technologies provides cloud-based business management software that helps organizations manage projects, customers, employees, finances, and reporting through a secure SaaS platform.

---

# Customer Support Policy

Our support team is available Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (UTC).

Priority Levels:

Priority 1
- Complete service outage
- Data loss
- Security incidents

Priority 2
- Application crashes
- Login failures
- Major functionality unavailable

Priority 3
- Feature requests
- General product questions
- Documentation assistance

---

# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchasing an annual subscription.

Refund requests are reviewed manually.

Refund approval is never automated.

Processing time:
5–7 business days after approval.

---

# Subscription Cancellation

Customers may cancel their subscription at any time.

Cancellation requests require supervisor approval before processing.

Subscriptions remain active until the end of the current billing cycle.

---

# Account Closure

Customers requesting permanent account deletion must undergo identity verification.

Account closure requests require human approval.

Deleted accounts cannot be restored.

---

# Compensation Policy

Requests involving compensation, service credits, or financial reimbursement require approval from Customer Success Management.

---

# Data Privacy

ABC Technologies follows industry-standard security practices.

Customer information is encrypted both in transit and at rest.

Passwords are never stored in plaintext.

---

# Escalation Policy

The following requests must always be escalated to a human supervisor:

- Refund requests
- Subscription cancellation
- Account closure
- Compensation requests
- Escalation to management
